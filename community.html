How do I join a club or team?

Browse the teams section, pick the one you like and hit "Join". The organizer will receive your request.

Are athletes verified?

Yes. Every athlete and coach profile is reviewed and verified by the Prime team.

Can I create my own team?

Yes. Sign in, open your profile and create a group. You can invite players and organize matches.

Is there a cost to join the community?

Joining the community is free. Some teams and coaches charge their own fees, shown on each card.
